What am I looking at?

A virtual square plate has resonant modes φ = cos(nπx)cos(mπy) − s·cos(mπx)cos(nπy) at f ≈ F₀(n²+m²). Each mode is driven by the sound energy near its own resonance, and the sand slides toward the nodal lines, as on a real Chladni plate. Loudness (dB) sets how hard the plate is driven.
