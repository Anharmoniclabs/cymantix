CYMANTIX

A Chladni sand plate driven by whatever you play.

Pick a source to start, or drag across the plate to bow it. Nothing leaves your browser.
What am I looking at?

Incoming audio drives a modeled 1 mm steel plate, each mode a damped oscillator at the audio sample rate. The plate is tuned to the music: the dominant pitch picks a figure, low notes simple ones and high notes intricate ones, and the sand gathers on its nodal lines.

The square has free edges; the circular plate has a clamped edge. This is a numerically defined virtual plate, not a measurement of a physical one. The sand transport is approximate. Model, limits & verification.
