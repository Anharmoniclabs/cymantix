How the plate responds

Sound excites the simulated plate’s resonances. Grains move toward its nodal lines, where vibration is weakest. Square and circular plates have different resonances.

The spectrum covers 20 Hz to the lower of 20 kHz or half the audio sample rate. Deep bass uses a longer window; upper frequencies and transients use faster analysis. Both stereo channels are measured.

Sounds outside the available plate resonances still agitate the sand, but do not create a fabricated resonance. This is a visual simulation, not a calibrated vibration sensor. Input hardware determines what can actually be captured.

Microphone mode requests echo cancellation, noise suppression, and automatic gain control off. Some devices may still apply processing. dBFS describes digital signal level, not room loudness in dB SPL.
