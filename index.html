Model & limits

Waveform → damped modal equations → full acceleration covariance → spatial vibration field → grains. Square modes use a converged Ritz solution; circular modes satisfy free-edge plate boundary conditions.

Young’s modulus 200 GPa; density 7,850 kg/m³; Poisson ratio 0.3; damping ratio 0.015. Fixed off-centre actuator: 1 N at full digital scale. Modes retained to 7.5 kHz; rigid translation and tilt excluded.

Each display factor combines all retained modes. Up to 24 factors are used, with residual energy and approximation reported. Sand uses a bounded migration and contact model; it is not a resolved grain collision simulation.
